The main short vowels in Arabic:  
َ: فَتحَة : Fat-ha : A sound  
ِ: كَسرَة : Kasra : I and E sound  
ُ: ضَمَّة : Dhamma : U sound  
ْ: سُكُون : Consonant sound Sukuun  
Ta’a Marboota (ة): This is the main marker for feminine in Arabic. It comes only at the end of the word with nouns and adjectives . This letter we don’t pronounce, in general, except in some cases we have to pronounce it. We will cover that later.

*Pseudo verbs:*  
The pseudo verbs are not real verbs, they are derived from prepositions or other words in Arabic. Even though they are not real, they still function as regular verbs. Also, we negate them as regular verbs.

The auxiliary : To have with , with : مَع  
We use the auxiliary “Ma’” when we want to talk about something or someone that we have it or him with us right now, at the moment of speaking.
